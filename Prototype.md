# Prototype Development

## Objective

A physical demonstration prototype was developed to represent the proposed multi-layer Guava Guard liner.

## Materials Used for Demonstration

The prototype was constructed using easily available materials to represent the proposed structure:

- Cardboard box for the crate model
- Foam sheet to represent the ethylene-scavenging layer
- Kitchen towel / porous sheet to represent the cellulose-based porous layer
- Cardboard support layer
- Adhesive and basic craft materials

## Prototype Construction

1. A cardboard box was modified to represent a fruit crate.
2. A base support layer was prepared.
3. A foam sheet was used as a visual representation of the proposed biochar layer.
4. A porous kitchen towel was placed above the representative scavenging layer.
5. The layers were assembled to demonstrate the proposed liner structure.
6. The completed liner was positioned inside the crate model.

## Important Note

The materials used in this prototype are demonstration substitutes and do not represent the final commercial formulation.

The final product is proposed to use rice husk biochar and a suitable biodegradable cellulose-based material.

## Current Status

The prototype demonstrates the physical arrangement, placement and working concept of Guava Guard. Laboratory testing is required to validate ethylene adsorption, moisture management and shelf-life extension.
